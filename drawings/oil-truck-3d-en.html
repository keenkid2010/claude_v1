    Layout notes

    • IBC tote (1,000 kg full) at the rear door against the driver-side wall, exchanged by forklift through the full-width rear door. Quick-connect hose to the purifier; locating stops and ratchet straps.
    • Vacuum oil purifier (DZL-30A, approx. 16 kW, DN32 connections) rotated 90° so the 1,100 mm operation side (control cabinet, Roots and vacuum pumps) faces the aisle. Casters removed, bolted to the channel base, lifting lugs cut after installation. Vacuum pump exhaust piped outside.
    • Generator room at the cab end (66 kW genset): 50 mm fire-rated partition with double access doors to the genset service side, 400 mm service gap, louvered service doors at both ends (cooling air in and out), exhaust stack through the roof with rain cap.
    • Walkways: main walkway on the curb side 890 mm (35.0 in), rear walkway 660 mm (26.0 in); both meet the 24 in minimum.
    • Electrical panel (IP54) and oil connection box (3 capped DN32 ports + grounding stud) both on the driver side with exterior access. E-stop inside the side door.
    • Side personnel door 800 mm, out-swing, on the curb side 1,850 mm from the rear wall, with fold-down aluminum ladder.
    • Also: underbody diesel tank on the curb side; enclosed tool & parts rack (280 mm deep) on the curb wall; explosion-proof exhaust fan in the oil work area; three 4 kg dry chemical extinguishers.
